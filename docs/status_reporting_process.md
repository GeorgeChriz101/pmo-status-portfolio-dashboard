# Weekly Status Reporting Process

## 1. Collect Updates

Gather current site and milestone information.

## 2. Validate Data

Review missing owners, missing dates, and inconsistent site identifiers.

## 3. Calculate Metrics

Update completion, overdue, issue, and portfolio totals.

## 4. Review Risks

Identify sites requiring management attention.

## 5. Review Upcoming Work

Confirm near-term milestones, owners, and dependencies.

## 6. Confirm Issue Ownership

Make sure each active issue has a responsible owner.

## 7. Identify Decisions

Document stakeholder decisions required to keep work moving.

## 8. Reconcile Totals

Confirm raw and processed totals match.

## 9. Prepare Weekly Reporting

Update the Excel workbook, dashboard datasets, and PowerPoint status deck.
